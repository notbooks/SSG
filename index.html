HOW TO SMASH GUNT

• Damage → Knockback: at 0% a punch tickles. At 120% the same punch sends you to the blast zone.
• Stocks: get knocked off stage past the blast zone = lose a stock. Last standing wins.
• Tech: Double-jump, wall-jump (near edge), shield, air-dodge, fast-fall (hold down in air), GUNT projectile.
• Attacks: Jab (quick), Smash (hold + direction = KO), Aerials, Up/Down Smash, Gun shot.
• Drop a .gltf or .glb into /assets and it appears in the skin selector automatically (fetch at boot).
